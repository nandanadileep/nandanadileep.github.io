MARCUS AURELIUS · MEDITATIONS, BOOKS I–XII

Meditations

One of the best books I've ever read.

Mark as medication

How to be

  • no meddling. no gossip. no getting lost in tiny, useless things.
  • not every clever debate deserves an answer. especially the ones meant to make me look clever.
  • less announcing the philosophy. more quietly living it. plain clothes, plain life.
  • talk less. forgive quickly. leave room for someone to speak plainly to me.
  • the same person in pain, illness, loss, praise, success. steady purpose.
  • knowing more is never permission to embarrass someone. correct gently. sometimes just use the right word back.
  • busy is not an excuse for being cold. and criticism from someone close? listen before defending.
  • no envy. no double face. no performance of goodness.
  • integrity before comfort, status, power. if power comes: still truthful, humane, composed.
  • strong and gentle at once. authority without hardness. kindness without weakness.

The inside

  • the ruling mind, the part that chooses. this is the part to protect.
  • my peace was not placed in somebody else's hands. thankfully.
  • watch the movement of my own mind. much more useful than guessing what everyone else thinks.
  • the event, then the story I tell about the event. usually the second thing hurts more.
  • also allowed: no opinion yet.
  • pleasure and pain can report what is happening. they don't get to rule.
  • pain has edges. imagination keeps trying to remove them. don't help it.
  • drop “I am hurt” and see what is actually left.

Today, specifically

  • this task. this hour. not the old scene, not the imagined disaster.
  • do less, properly. keep asking: is this one of the unnecessary things?
  • action over activity. a full calendar can still be an empty day.
  • do it with a purpose. do it as if there may not be another turn.
  • time does not come back. some urgency, then. but no panic.
  • learn one good thing. keeps the mind from scattering.
  • enough discussion of the good person. do the next good thing.

Change, fate, death

  • everything changing into something else. not a defect in the universe. the method.
  • if ordered, trust it. if chaotic, I can still keep my character.
  • life like breath. taken in, given back. never owned.
  • three days, three generations. from far enough away, almost no difference.
  • body back to elements. name back to silence. ordinary natural processes.
  • luxury, fame, the whole physical display. very perishable things.
  • occasionally rehearse losing what I love. not to become grim. to remember it is here now.

Other people

  • morning reminder: busybodies, arrogance, ingratitude, deceit. mostly confusion about what is good.
  • their behaviour is theirs. keeping my character uncorrupted is mine.
  • wrong belief behind the wrong act. easier to feel pity than rage.
  • people are for one another. gently teach, or patiently bear with.
  • show me I am wrong and I should change gladly. truth did not injure me.
  • the accidental scratch in the gym. no moral drama. much of life like this.
  • don't borrow the eyes of the person trying to hurt me. look at the thing itself.
  • no curiosity about lives that are not mine, unless it helps the common good.
  • anger usually punishes me for someone else's mistake. bad trade.

Applause

  • the famous person, the admirer, the memory of both. gone soon enough.
  • an emerald does not improve when praised. neither does a good act.
  • do the useful thing and move on. no keeping score, no secret invoice.
  • posthumous fame: being remembered by people who will also disappear.
  • tie the good to my own acts and understanding. not to their reaction.

What gets in the way

  • the mind keeps turning obstacles into material. what blocks the road becomes road.
  • if it cannot make me unjust, dishonest, cowardly, uncontrolled, how exactly has it ruined me?
  • not punishment. maybe a prescription. something to practise against.
  • stumbled again. return again. the returning matters more than the spotless streak.
  • real misfortune: the thing that stops right action. surprisingly short list.

A simple life

  • just thoughts. useful action. no lying. no quarrel with what has already arrived.
  • justice, truth, temperance, courage. enough of a direction.
  • escape my own wickedness. actually possible. escaping everyone else's? not.
  • after a good deed, don't wait around for the third reward: recognition.
  • having less is not poverty. endlessly wanting more probably is.
  • the body eats, sleeps, breathes, aches. ordinary machinery. point it toward proper work.
  • pleasure is not proof of goodness. desire makes a terrible master.
  • a steady life still available. think well, act well, begin again.
